Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

1. Data Controller

The data controller for your personal data is Lumera. For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at lumera0000@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

  • Account data: email address, username, encrypted password
  • Usage data: course progress, quiz results, learning streak
  • Payment data: handled by Stripe — we never store card details
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, session duration

3. Purpose and Legal Basis

  • Providing the educational platform service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — contract performance)
  • Processing payments and subscriptions (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • Security and fraud prevention (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest)
  • Sending newsletters if you have given consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)

4. Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. Upon deletion, data is removed within 30 days, except where required by tax law (5 years).

5. Data Sharing

We only share data with trusted processors necessary to operate the service:

  • Supabase — database hosting (EU servers)
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Vercel — application hosting
  • Clerk — user authentication

We never sell your personal data to any third party.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

  • Right of access to your data
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority

To exercise your rights: lumera0000@gmail.com

7. Cookies

We use only essential cookies for session management and authentication. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes by email or in-app notification with at least 14 days' notice.
